Our story

The Citrus Company started with a simple idea: skincare shouldn’t feel complicated. Too many steps, too many labels, too much noise.

So we built a small range around one bright ingredient, lemon extract, paired with gentle botanicals, aloe, shea butter and vitamins. Each product does one job well and looks good doing it.

From a face wash in the morning to a lip balm at night, everything is made to be reached for every day.
